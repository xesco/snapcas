# Gradiente Expert XP-800 (MSX1, 64 KB) with an SD Mapper v1. The XP-800 is a
# Brazilian MSX1. The SD Mapper v1, by Fabio Belavenuto, brings its 512 KB memory
# mapper and Nextor on its SD cards. openMSX needs its firmware, SDMAPPER.ROM 1.0.7,
# which the Makefile downloads. The disk goes in its first SD card slot.
openmsx -machine Gradiente_Expert_XP-800 -ext SD_Mapper_v1
disk hda
variant xp800
firmware systemroms/snapcas/SDMAPPER.ROM
