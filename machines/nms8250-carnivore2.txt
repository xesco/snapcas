# Philips NMS 8250 (MSX2, 128 KB) with a Carnivore2. The NMS 8250's 128 KB is a
# memory mapper, and it has a floppy drive. The Carnivore2 runs Nextor, with the
# cartridge's 1 MB mapper and its disk interface.

disk hda
