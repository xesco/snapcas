# Philips VG-8020 (MSX1, 64 KB) with an SD Mapper v1. The SD Mapper v1, by Fabio
# Belavenuto, brings its 512 KB memory mapper and Nextor on its SD cards. openMSX
# needs its firmware, SDMAPPER.ROM 1.0.7, which the Makefile downloads. The disk
# goes in its first SD card slot.
openmsx -machine Philips_VG_8020 -ext SD_Mapper_v1
disk hda
variant vg8020
