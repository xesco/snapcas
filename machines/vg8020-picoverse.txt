# A Philips VG-8020 with a cartridge like the PicoVerse: a Sunrise IDE interface
# under Nextor and a 192 KB memory mapper. openMSX has no PicoVerse, so two
# cartridges stand in for it: SunriseIDE_Nextor and ram192k, which the Makefile
# makes from openMSX's ram512k.
openmsx -machine Philips_VG_8020 -ext SunriseIDE_Nextor -ext ram192k
disk hda
variant vg8020
