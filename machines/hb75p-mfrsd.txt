# Sony HB-75P, an MSX1 with 64 KB, with a MegaFlashROM SCC+ SD in openMSX's
# stock setup: it boots MSX-DOS from the ROM disk in its flash, as drive A:, and
# its memory mapper and Nextor serve the images. The disk goes in its first SD
# card slot, drive B:.
openmsx -machine Sony_HB-75P -ext MegaFlashROM_SCC+_SD
disk hda
dos "A:\>" B
