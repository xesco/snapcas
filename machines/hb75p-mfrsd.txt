# Sony HB-75P (MSX1, 64 KB) with a MegaFlashROM SCC+ SD. The cartridge has openMSX's
# stock setup: it boots MSX-DOS from the ROM disk in its flash, as drive A:, and its
# memory mapper and Nextor serve the images. The disk goes in its first SD card
# slot, drive B:.
openmsx -machine Sony_HB-75P -ext MegaFlashROM_SCC+_SD
disk hda
dos "A:\>" B
variant hb75p
