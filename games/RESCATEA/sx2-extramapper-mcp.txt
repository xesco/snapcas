# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so nothing calls SX2RES. The image's wrapper
# at FCC9h, before the tape service's entries, calls SX2RES and jumps to a RET
# at FC5Fh.
rescall FC5F

# An entry at FC4Ch, in BASIC's zeros of TRPTBL, which the game leaves alone,
# counts its calls in the byte at FC60h and calls the wrapper at every 64th,
# then does LD IX,(5582h) and returns.
patch page3:3C4C 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 3A 60 FC C6 04 32 60 FC 30
patch page3:3C56 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 03 CD C9 FC F1 DD 2A 82 55 C9

# The routine at 53E5h reads the game's keys, about 3000 times a second in the
# title and in play. It calls the entry in place of LD IX,(5582h), about 45
# times a second.
patch page1:13E7 DD 2A 82 55 > CD 4C FC 00
