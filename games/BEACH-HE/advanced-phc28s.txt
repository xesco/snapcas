# Lines for the Sanyo PHC-28S with a RAM cartridge in slot 1 and a Carnivore2 in slot 2.

# The capture runs without the Carnivore2, so that page 3 is the internal RAM in
# slot 0, as on a PHC-28S without it.
capture advanced/phc28s-nodisk

# beach1's routine at 87D8h saves the slot register on the stack, selects page 3's
# slot in pages 1 to 3 to copy 8800h to 4000h, and then pops the saved value. It
# assumes that page 3's slot holds pages 1 and 2, and for page 3 in slot 0 or 1 it
# takes 54h, slot 1 in pages 1 to 3. On the PHC-28S that moves page 3, and the
# stack with it, to the RAM cartridge, so the pop reads other bytes and the load
# stops halfway. 14h selects the RAM cartridge in pages 1 and 2 only.
patch side1:158 0E 54 79 D3 A8 > 0E 14 79 D3 A8
