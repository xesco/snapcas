# Lines for hbf700p-ram512k-msxdos2, the Sony HB-F700P (MSX2, 256 KB) with a 512 KB
# RAM cartridge, under MSX-DOS 2.

# The game selects its RAM in slot 2 by fixed values, as on the Toshiba HX-10. The
# HB-F700P has the MSX-DOS 2 cartridge in slot 2, whose ROM is in page 1. The
# capture's slot patch replaces only a field that names a slot that holds nothing,
# so it left slot 2 in page 1, and the loader copied the game's page 1 into the
# ROM. These patches select slot 1, the RAM cartridge, in place of slot 2.
# LD A,0AAh / OUT (0A8h),A selects slot 2 in every page, 0A8h the ROM in page 0
# and slot 2 in pages 1 to 3, and 0A0h the ROM in pages 0 and 1 and slot 2 in
# pages 2 and 3. The new values are 55h, 54h and 50h.
