# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game takes the interrupt in routines of its own through H.KEYI: JP 84D3h
# at the snapshot, which the image chains after its CALL resb, and from play
# on JP 0ADD4h, which its code at 8B15h writes a byte at a time, and JP 84D3h
# again from 8F89h, both over the image's call. So SX2RES got no more calls.
# The image gets a wrapper for each that calls SX2RES and jumps to the
# routine, at FD00h for ADD4h and FD06h for 84D3h, and each LD A,n of the
# address takes the wrapper's bytes.
rescall ADD4 84D3
patch page2:0B1C D4 > 00
patch page2:0B21 AD > FD
patch page2:0F8F D3 > 06
patch page2:0F94 84 > FD
