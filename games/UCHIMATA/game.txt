# The game's loader, at 86B2h and copied to 400Bh, reads 5808h bytes into 85D9h
# with TAPIN after its own TAPION, but the tape holds 22152 of them, in a block of
# 22144 and one of 8, and ends there. The loader then waits for the rest, with its
# interrupts off and a blank screen, in a plain load in openMSX as well. The
# dump's last block ends in zeros, so the missing bytes are taken to be its
# padding. The patch makes the count 5688h, the 22152 bytes the tape holds, after
# which the loader stops the motor and starts the game at 4230h.
patch side1:2018 01 08 58 > 01 88 56
