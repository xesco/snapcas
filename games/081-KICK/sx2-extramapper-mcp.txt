# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The capture runs on sx2-extramapper-noram30, the SX-2 with its Extra-Mapper on and
# without the 2048 KB mapper in slot 3-0. On sx2-extramapper-mcp itself the game
# fails: build.py: the snapshot keeps the RAM page 3-0-0-3.bin in slot 3-0, which
# the machine's resident program holds: the game needs RAM in another slot. `make
# games` wrote this line.
capture sx2-extramapper-noram30

# After the snapshot the game puts its own routine into H.KEYI, over the
# image's call of SX2RES: JP 4639h for its music, copied from 48E2h, which
# chains the hook that it saved, and at play its routine at 449Ch, copied from
# 4499h, which calls the hook that it saved at 405Eh. The image gets a wrapper
# that calls SX2RES and jumps to 4639h, at FD60h, and the template's JP 4639h
# becomes JP 0FD60h, as in KICK-IT's file.
rescall 4639
patch page1:08E3 39 46 > 60 FD
