# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The game sets its stack at the top of memory, with LD SP,0000h. Its first
# push writes FFFFh, which is the subslot register of slot 0 here, where the
# RAM is in 0-1, and the write deselects the RAM. An MSX2+'s BIOS also keeps
# its copies of VDP registers 23 and 25 to 27 in FFF6h and FFFAh to FFFCh, and
# the main ROM's slot at FFF7h. The patch sets the stack below all of them, at
# FFE7h, as on sx2.
patch side1:129 31 00 00 > 31 E7 FF
