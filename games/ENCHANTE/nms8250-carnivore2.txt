# Lines for the Philips NMS 8250 with a Carnivore2 in slot 1.

# The game copies its character set to EA60h to FFFFh, by an LDIR of 15A0h
# bytes. On a machine whose RAM is in an expanded slot, FFFFh is the slot's
# subslot register, and the copy's last byte deselects the RAM. The NMS 8250
# has no other RAM. The patch makes the copy 159Fh bytes. The byte left out is
# the last row of the last character, a row of 00h, and the game then reads the
# subslot register there.
patch side1:3BDD 01 A0 15 > 01 9F 15
