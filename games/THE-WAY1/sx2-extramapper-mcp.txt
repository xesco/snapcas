# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so nothing calls SX2RES. The image's code
# for the call, its call routine res3 and its wrapper at FD56h, which calls res3
# and jumps to a RET at FCDCh, goes to FD29h, in SLTWRK's zeros of the empty
# slots 1 and 2.
rescode FD29
rescall FCDC

# Two entries at FCC9h, in SLTATR's zeros, count their calls in the byte after
# them and call the wrapper at every 256th of the first and every 64th of the
# second, then do the instructions that their calls replace and return.
patch page3:3CC9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 3A F1 FC C6 01 32 F1 FC 30
patch page3:3CD3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 03 CD 56 FD F1 DB AA E6 F0 C9
patch page3:3CDD 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 3A F1 FC C6 04 32 F1 FC 30
patch page3:3CE7 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 03 CD 56 FD F1 06 64 10 FE C9

# The title waits for a key in a loop from 5F70h that reads the keyboard's
# rows, about 5000 times a second. It calls the first entry in place of
# IN A,(0AAh) / AND 0F0h, about 20 times a second.
patch page1:1F7F DB AA E6 F0 > CD C9 FC 00

# The menu waits for 1 to 4 in a loop from 5C3Dh that starts with a delay,
# LD B,64h / DJNZ $, about 2300 times a second. It calls the second entry in
# place of the delay, about 35 times a second. The fights load from the other
# tapes.
patch page1:1C3D 06 64 10 FE > CD DD FC 00
