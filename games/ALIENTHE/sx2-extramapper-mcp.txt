# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The capture runs on sx2-extramapper-noram30, the SX-2 with its Extra-Mapper on and
# without the 2048 KB mapper in slot 3-0. On sx2-extramapper-mcp itself the game
# fails: build.py: the snapshot keeps the RAM page 3-0-1-2.bin in slot 3-0, which
# the machine's resident program holds: the game needs RAM in another slot. `make
# games` wrote this line.
capture sx2-extramapper-noram30

# Interrupts are off and H.KEYI holds game data, so place a direct-I/O SX2RES
# caller (slot 3-0 4040h, F5h DBh check) in unused page 2 zeros at BD40h. The
# entry at BD9Dh calls it on keyboard row 0 (~68 Hz), then runs LD C,A / IN A,(0AAh).
patch page2:3D40 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 C5 D5 E5 DD E5 FD E5 D9 C5
patch page2:3D4A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D5 E5 08 F5 08 ED 57 F5 F3 DB
patch page2:3D54 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > A8 47 F6 C0 D3 A8 3A FF FF 2F
patch page2:3D5E 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 4F E6 F3 32 FF FF 78 E6 F3 F6
patch page2:3D68 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 0C D3 A8 C5 3A 40 40 FE F5 20
patch page2:3D72 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 08 3A 41 40 FE DB CC 40 40 C1
patch page2:3D7C 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 78 F6 C0 D3 A8 79 32 FF FF 78
patch page2:3D86 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D3 A8 F1 E2 8D BD FB 08 F1 08
patch page2:3D90 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 D1 C1 D9 FD E1 DD E1 E1 D1
patch page2:3D9A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > C1 F1 C9 B7 CC 40 BD 4F DB AA
patch page2:3DA4 00 > C9

# The game reads each row of the keyboard through the routine at 5526h, many
# times a second, in the title and in play. It calls the entry at BD9Dh in place
# of those instructions.
patch page1:1526 4F DB AA > CD 9D BD
