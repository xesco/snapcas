# Lines for sx2, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 2048 KB) as it starts, with its slot 0 expanded.

# The game sets its stack at the top of memory, with LD SP,0000h in three
# places. Its first push writes FFFFh, which is the subslot register of the
# SX-2's RAM, and the write deselects the RAM. An MSX2+'s BIOS also keeps its
# copies of VDP registers 23 and 25 to 27 in FFF6h and FFFAh to FFFCh, and the
# main ROM's slot at FFF7h. The patches set the stack below all of them, at
# FFE7h.
patch side1:1D70 31 00 00 > 31 E7 FF
patch side1:1D8A 31 00 00 > 31 E7 FF
patch side1:2728 31 00 00 > 31 E7 FF
