# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The loader AT, at 9C40h, looks for RAM in pages 0 and 1 by the slot register
# alone, and keeps the value at 9C98h. Here the RAM is in slot 0-1, beside the
# BIOS in 0-0, so the search finds none, or SX2RES's mapper in slot 3-0. The
# patch makes the search return at once, and the patches below select the RAM
# by slot 0's subslot register instead.
patch side1:253 DB A8 > FB C9

# The routine at 9CB7h copies a block to page 0 or 1 with the value at 9C98h,
# and gives back the old value. The patch rewrites it to write 55h, RAM in
# every page, for the copy, and 50h, BASIC's subslots, after it.
patch side1:2C5 DB A8 F5 3A 98 9C D3 A8 21 00 > 3E 55 32 FF FF 21 00 A0 01 00
patch side1:2CF A0 01 00 40 ED B0 F1 D3 A8 C9 > 40 ED B0 3E 50 32 FF FF C9 00

# The last routine, at 9CD4h, and its copy in the game at F425h, select the
# RAM in every page with that value. The patches write 55h instead.
patch side1:2E3 3A 98 9C D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:B9AB 3A 98 9C D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF

# The last routine moves A000h to DFFFh to C000h to FFFFh, by an LDDR from
# FFFFh. FFFFh is slot 0's subslot register here, and the write deselects the
# RAM. The patch starts the move at FFFEh, one byte shorter.
patch side1:2E8 11 FF FF 21 FF DF 01 00 40 > 11 FE FF 21 FE DF 01 FF 3F

# The game's image has no call of SX2RES, and the game writes over the BIOS's
# slot routines and tables in page 3. A routine at A410h, in zeros of page 2
# that the game leaves alone, uses no BIOS routine. It keeps all the registers
# and the interrupts, selects slot 3's subslot 0 in page 1 with slot 3's subslot
# register, selects slot 3 in page 1 with the slot register, and calls SX2RES's
# entry at 4040h when it starts F5h DBh. Then it gives both registers back. The
# routine runs in page 2, because it moves pages 1 and 3. An entry at A46Dh
# takes a stack below A4F8h, in the same zeros, calls the routine, takes the
# game's stack back, then does LD A,05h / OUT (0AAh),A and returns.
patch page2:2410 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 C5 D5 E5 DD E5 FD E5 D9 C5
patch page2:241A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D5 E5 08 F5 08 ED 57 F5 F3 DB
patch page2:2424 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > A8 47 F6 C0 D3 A8 3A FF FF 2F
patch page2:242E 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 4F E6 F3 32 FF FF 78 E6 F3 F6
patch page2:2438 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 0C D3 A8 C5 3A 40 40 FE F5 20
patch page2:2442 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 08 3A 41 40 FE DB CC 40 40 C1
patch page2:244C 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 78 F6 C0 D3 A8 79 32 FF FF 78
patch page2:2456 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D3 A8 F1 E2 5D A4 FB 08 F1 08
patch page2:2460 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 D1 C1 D9 FD E1 DD E1 E1 D1
patch page2:246A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > C1 F1 C9 ED 73 80 A4 31 F8 A4
patch page2:2474 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > CD 10 A4 ED 7B 80 A4 3E 05 D3

# The game reads the keyboard's rows 5, 4 and 3 in a loop from 6470h, about 40
# times a second. Its stack is in page 1, which the call moves. It calls the
# entry at A46Dh in place of those instructions.
