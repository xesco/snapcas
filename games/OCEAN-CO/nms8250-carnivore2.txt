# Lines for the Philips NMS 8250 with a Carnivore2 in slot 1.

# The loader moves the game's last part to C000h to FFFFh, by an LDDR of 4000h
# bytes. On a machine whose RAM is in an expanded slot, FFFFh is the slot's
# subslot register, and the move's first byte deselects the RAM. The NMS 8250
# has no other RAM. The patch makes the move 3FFFh bytes, from DFFEh to FFFEh.
# The byte left out is the last row of the character set's last character, a
# row of 00h, and the game then reads the subslot register there.
patch side1:2E8 11 FF FF 21 FF DF 01 00 40 > 11 FE FF 21 FE DF 01 FF 3F
