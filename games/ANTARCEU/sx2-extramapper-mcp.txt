# on and SX2RES running.

# The game's start at 4010h fills FD00h to FEFFh with RET and then puts JP 405Dh
# in H.KEYI, so at the snapshot page 3 has no room for the call of SX2RES: the
# image has none. FD29h to FD68h are SLTWRK's entries of slots 1 and 2, empty
# on the SX-2, and nothing reads the RETs there. The patch gives them back their
# zeros, where the build puts the call, and H.KEYI calls SX2RES and then jumps
# to 405Dh.
