# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The game looks for RAM in page 1 with WSLREG in primary slots 1, 2 and 3 and
# gives up when it finds none. Here the RAM is the Extra-Mapper in 0-1, which
# shares slot 0 with the BIOS. After the game keeps the slot register at 87ADh,
# the patch at 8672h writes 54h to slot 0's subslot register, the BIOS in page
# 0 and the Extra-Mapper in pages 1 to 3, and jumps to 86A0h, where the search
# goes on when it finds RAM.
patch side1:2B19 CD 38 01 32 AD 87 E6 F0 > CD 38 01 32 AD 87 3E 54
patch side1:2B21 47 F6 04 CD 8F > 32 FF FF 18 27

# The game runs in interrupt mode 2 with a table at 8000h that sends every
# interrupt to F1F1h, where its init puts JP 0C604h, its interrupt routine. So
# the image's wrapper at FD56h calls SX2RES and jumps to that routine, and the
# patch below sends the interrupt to the wrapper.
rescall C604

# The init copies JP 0C604h from 87AFh to F1F1h. Its address becomes FD56h.
patch side1:2C5D 04 C6 > 56 FD

# The title runs without interrupts, so the image's wrapper of the game's
# interrupt routine never runs there. A routine at FCD9h, in the zeros of SLTATR
# and SLTWRK, reads the VDP's status register 0, which the game never reads
# there. When its bit 7 shows a new frame, it keeps IX, IY and the alternate
# registers and calls the image's routine at FD29h, res3, so at most once a
# frame. An entry at FCFCh calls it and jumps to the BIOS's SNSMAT.
patch page3:3CD9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 DB 99 07 30 1B DD E5 FD E5
patch page3:3CE3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D9 C5 D5 E5 08 F5 08 D9 CD 29
patch page3:3CED 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > FD D9 08 F1 08 E1 D1 C1 D9 FD
patch page3:3CF7 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 DD E1 F1 C9 CD D9 FC C3 41
patch page3:3D01 00 > 01

# The title waits for 1 or 2 in a loop that reads the keyboard's row 0 with
# SNSMAT at B164h. It calls the entry above in place of SNSMAT.
patch page2:3164 CD 41 01 > CD FC FC
