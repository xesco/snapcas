# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# Each of the two loaders, at C802h, finds the Extra-Mapper in slot 0-1 for
# pages 0 and 1, writes slot 0's subslot register for its copies, and starts
# the ROM image at the word at 4002h. The ROM image looks up its own subslot in
# SLTTBL at FCC5h, still BASIC's 50h, and gives page 2 subslot 0 of slot 0,
# where nothing is. The patches put a routine in free bytes at C930h that writes
# both FFFFh and FCC5h, and call it in place of the three writes of FFFFh.
patch side1:4279 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 32 FF FF 32 C5 FC C9
patch side1:83FE 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 32 FF FF 32 C5 FC C9
patch side1:4251 32 FF FF > CD 30 C9
patch side1:425E 32 FF FF > CD 30 C9
patch side1:426B 32 FF FF > CD 30 C9
patch side1:83D6 32 FF FF > CD 30 C9
patch side1:83E3 32 FF FF > CD 30 C9
patch side1:83F0 32 FF FF > CD 30 C9

# When a key starts the game, it copies three RETs into H.KEYI from DCDCh,
# over the image's CALL resb, so SX2RES got no more calls.
# The BIOS's interrupt routine still calls H.TIMI after it, so H.TIMI calls
# resb at FD29h too: CALL 0FD29h / RET.
patch page3:3D9F C9 8B E4 40 C9 > CD 29 FD C9 C9
