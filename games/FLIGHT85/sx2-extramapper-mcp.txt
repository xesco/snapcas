# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game's title runs without interrupts, so the image's call of SX2RES from
# H.KEYI runs only in play. A routine at FCD9h, in the zeros of SLTATR and
# SLTWRK, reads the VDP's status register 0, which the game never reads there.
# When its bit 7 shows a new frame, it keeps IX, IY and the alternate registers
# and calls the image's routine at FD29h, so at most once a frame. An entry at
# FCFCh calls it, then does IN A,(0AAh) / AND 0F0h and returns.
patch page3:3CD9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 DB 99 07 30 1B DD E5 FD E5
patch page3:3CE3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D9 C5 D5 E5 08 F5 08 D9 CD 29
patch page3:3CED 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > FD D9 08 F1 08 E1 D1 C1 D9 FD
patch page3:3CF7 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 DD E1 F1 C9 CD D9 FC DB AA
patch page3:3D01 00 00 00 > E6 F0 C9

# The title waits for a key of the keyboard's row 0 in a loop that reads the row
# from E9A0h. It calls the entry above in place of IN A,(0AAh) / AND 0F0h.
patch page3:29A0 DB AA E6 F0 > CD FC FC 00
