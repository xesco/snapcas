# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image has no call of SX2RES, and its
# stack is in page 1, where SX2RES's entry is, so the image's call routine would
# leave the call out. The rescall line gives the routine at FD29h a wrapper at
# FD56h that calls SX2RES and jumps to the RET at FD71h.
rescall FD71

# An entry at FD60h, in zeros of page 3 that the game leaves alone, keeps SP at
# FD72h, calls the wrapper with its stack in the zeros below FD29h, takes SP
# back and jumps to the game's routine at EF16h.
patch page3:3D60 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 73 72 FD 31 29 FD CD 56 FD
patch page3:3D6A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 7B 72 FD C3 16 EF C9

# The routine at EF06h calls the routine at EF16h, about 10 times a second in
# play. It calls the entry at FD60h in place of that routine.
patch page3:2F0C CD 16 EF > CD 60 FD
