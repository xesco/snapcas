# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The capture runs on sx2-extramapper-noram30, without the 2048 KB mapper in
# 3-0. On sx2-extramapper-mcp itself the snapshot keeps a RAM page in 3-0, which
# SX2RES holds.
capture sx2-extramapper-noram30

# The game resets the machine 119.37 seconds after the load command, to start
# its ROM image, and the BIOS's boot then sets up page 3 again, over the image's
# call of SX2RES. At 122.6 seconds the boot is done and the ROM image runs, so
# the snapshot at that moment keeps the call.
snapshot at 122.6

# The ROM image keeps the VDP's frame interrupt off, so the image's call of
# SX2RES from H.KEYI never runs. It reads the joystick with GTSTCK in one
# routine, at 54AEh, thousands of times a second while it waits for the fire
# button, and never reads the VDP's status. A routine at BE00h, in zeros after
# the ROM image, reads that status, and when its frame flag is set, at most
# once a frame, calls the image's routine at FD29h, which calls SX2RES with
# the BIOS's CALSLT. It runs the call on a stack of its own below BFF0h and
# keeps IX, IY, the alternate registers and the interrupt flag, which the
# BIOS's slot routines change. Then it jumps to GTSTCK.
patch page2:3E00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 DB 99 07 30 2E ED 73 FE BF
patch page2:3E0A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 31 F0 BF ED 57 F5 DD E5 FD E5
patch page2:3E14 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D9 C5 D5 E5 D9 08 F5 08 CD 29
patch page2:3E1E 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > FD 08 F1 08 D9 E1 D1 C1 D9 FD
patch page2:3E28 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 DD E1 F1 E2 30 BE FB ED 7B
patch page2:3E32 00 00 00 00 00 00 > FE BF F1 C3 D5 00

# The call of GTSTCK calls the routine instead.
patch page1:14AE CD D5 00 > CD 00 BE
