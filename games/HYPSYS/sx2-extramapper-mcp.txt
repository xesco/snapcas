# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image has no call of SX2RES. The
# rescall line gives the image's call routine at FD29h a wrapper at FD56h that
# calls SX2RES and jumps to the game's routine at B0C0h.
rescall B0C0

# In play the routine at C0ACh calls the routine at B0C0h five times in each
# pass, about 15 times a second. It calls the wrapper in place of that routine.
patch page3:B3 CD C0 B0 > CD 56 FD
