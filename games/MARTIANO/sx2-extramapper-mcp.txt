# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The loader at E000h first selects page 1's slot, BASIC's ROM in 0-0, in page
# 2 with ENASLT. Here that writes slot 0's subslot register and leaves no RAM
# in page 2, where the loader then copies its RAM search. Where slot 0 is not
# expanded the call writes the slot register alone, which the loader writes
# again next. The patch removes the call.
patch side1:48 CD 24 00 > 00 00 00

# The loader's two RAM searches, for page 1 and then for page 0, find the RAM
# in 0-1, but their result at FFFEh is 00h here, where the BIOS and the RAM
# share slot 0, so the loader's switches by the slot register alone do
# nothing. The patches skip both searches and make the two switches writes of
# slot 0's subslot register: 54h, RAM in pages 1 to 3, before the game's load,
# and 55h, RAM in every page, before its start at 4000h.
patch side1:D4 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 54 32 FF FF
patch side1:FD 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:27D 47 08 78 E6 > 4F 08 18 42
patch side1:2CD 47 08 78 E6 > 4F 08 18 3F

# In play the game takes the interrupt at its routine at 8D73h, but its stack is
# in page 1, where SX2RES's entry is, so the image's call routine leaves the
# call out. The rescall line gives the routine at FD29h a wrapper at FD56h that
# calls SX2RES and jumps to the RET at FD71h.
rescall FD71

# An entry at FD60h, in zeros of page 3 that the game leaves alone, keeps SP at
# FD72h, calls the wrapper with its stack in the zeros below FD29h, takes SP
# back and jumps to the game's interrupt routine at 8D73h.
patch page3:3D60 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 73 72 FD 31 29 FD CD 56 FD
patch page3:3D6A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 7B 72 FD C3 73 8D C9

# The jump at 0038h goes to the entry at FD60h in place of the game's interrupt
# routine, about 39 times a second in play.
patch page0:38 C3 73 8D > C3 60 FD
