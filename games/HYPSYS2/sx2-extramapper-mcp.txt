# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image has no call of SX2RES. The
# rescall line gives the image's call routine at FD29h two wrappers that call
# SX2RES: at FD56h, which jumps to the game's routine at B26Dh, and at FD5Ch,
# which jumps to its routine at B484h.
rescall B26D B484

# In play the game calls the routine at B26Dh from C215h, and its loop at BA7Ch
# calls the routine at B484h from BA85h, together about 16 times a second. Both
# call the wrappers in place of those routines.
patch page3:215 CD 6D B2 > CD 56 FD
patch page2:3A85 CD 84 B4 > CD 5C FD
