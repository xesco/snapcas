# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game takes the interrupt in its routine at 1792h, through JP at 0038h in
# its RAM page 0, and its code at 1780h writes that jump again when the play
# starts, over the jump to the image's wrapper. So the rescall line names the
# routine, and the patches make both jumps go to the wrapper at FCC9h.
rescall 1792

# The snapshot's JP 1792h at 0038h. With the rescall line's address, the build
# leaves that jump as it is.
patch page0:0039 92 17 > C9 FC

# LD HL,1792h before LD (0039h),HL, at the start of the play.
patch page0:1782 21 92 17 > 21 C9 FC
