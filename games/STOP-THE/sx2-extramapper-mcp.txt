# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game turns the VDP's interrupt off, so the image's call of SX2RES from
# T0 runs once. The routine at 9A29h copies 448 bytes of the game's tables
# once in each frame of the play, about 35 times a second. Its first
# instruction, LD HL,0E382h, becomes a jump to the image's wrapper at FCC9h,
# which calls SX2RES and jumps to the rest at F9A0h: LD HL,0E382h / JP 9A2Ch.
# The wrapper disables the interrupts, which the game does not take.
rescall F9A0

# LD HL,0E382h / JP 9A2Ch, in the BIOS's PLAY queues, zeros that the game
# leaves alone.
patch page3:39A0 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 21 82 E3 C3 2C 9A

# The game's code comes after the snapshot, in the block of 16 KB that its
# loader at EF19h reads to 8000h: each byte XOR the low byte of its address,
# and after each 256 bytes the count of their 1 bits. LD HL,0E382h at 9A29h
# becomes JP 0FCC9h, and the count of its 256 bytes changes with it.
patch side1:1C9F 08 A8 C8 > EA E3 D7
patch side1:1D76 97 > 9C
