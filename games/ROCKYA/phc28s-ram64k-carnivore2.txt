# Lines for the Sanyo PHC-28S with a RAM cartridge in slot 1 and a Carnivore2 in slot 2.

# The game selects RAM in slot 3. With Nextor, the PHC-28S has BASIC's pages 2
# and 3 in the Carnivore2's mapper, slot 2, and the RAM cartridge in slot 1.
# LD A,0FFh / OUT (0A8h),A selects slot 3 in pages 0 to 3. The patch selects
# slot 1 in pages 0 and 1, and slot 2 in pages 2 and 3 there.
patch side1:6581 3E FF D3 A8 > 3E A5 D3 A8

# LD A,0F0h / OUT (0A8h),A selects slot 3 in pages 2 and 3. The patch selects
# slot 2 there.
patch side1:6595 3E F0 D3 A8 > 3E A0 D3 A8

# LD A,0FFh / OUT (0A8h),A selects slot 3 in pages 0 to 3. The patch selects
# slot 1 in pages 0 and 1, and slot 2 in pages 2 and 3 there.
patch side1:AC49 3E FF D3 A8 > 3E A5 D3 A8

# LD A,0F0h / OUT (0A8h),A selects slot 3 in pages 2 and 3. The patch selects
# slot 2 there.
patch side1:AC5D 3E F0 D3 A8 > 3E A0 D3 A8
