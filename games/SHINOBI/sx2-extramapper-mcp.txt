# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so nothing calls SX2RES. The image's wrapper
# at FCC9h, before the tape service's entries, calls SX2RES and jumps to a RET
# at FC68h.
rescall FC68

# Two entries at FC4Ch, in BASIC's zeros of TRPTBL, which the game leaves alone,
# count their calls in the bytes from FC7Ch and call the wrapper at every 1024th
# of the first and every 128th of the second, then do the instructions that
# their calls replace and return.
patch page3:3C4C 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 3A 7C FC C6 01 32 7C FC 30
patch page3:3C56 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 0D 3A 7D FC C6 40 32 7D FC 30
patch page3:3C60 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 03 CD C9 FC F1 AF D3 AA C9 F5
patch page3:3C6A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 3A 7C FC C6 02 32 7C FC 30 03
patch page3:3C74 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > CD C9 FC F1 21 70 07 C9

# The title waits for 1, 2 or 3 in a loop at F102h that calls the key test at
# 0675h, about 27000 times a second. The test calls the first entry in place of
# XOR A / OUT (0AAh),A, about 25 times a second.
patch page0:675 AF D3 AA > CD 4C FC

# In play the routine at 0755h reads the game's keys, about 3800 times a second.
# It calls the second entry in place of LD HL,0770h, about 30 times a second.
patch page0:758 21 70 07 > CD 69 FC
