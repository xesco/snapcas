# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game's title runs without interrupts, so the image's wrapper of the game's
# interrupt routine calls SX2RES only in play. A routine at FCD9h, in the zeros
# of SLTATR and SLTWRK, does nothing more while the interrupts are on. Otherwise
# it reads the VDP's status register 0, and when its bit 7 shows a new frame, it
# keeps IX, IY and the alternate registers and calls the image's routine at
# FD29h, res3, so at most once a frame. Then it does IN A,(0AAh) / AND 0F0h and
# returns.
patch page3:3CD9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 ED 57 EA FF FC DB 99 07 30
patch page3:3CE3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 1B DD E5 FD E5 D9 C5 D5 E5 08
patch page3:3CED 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 08 D9 CD 29 FD D9 08 F1 08
patch page3:3CF7 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 D1 C1 D9 FD E1 DD E1 F1 DB
patch page3:3D01 00 00 00 00 > AA E6 F0 C9

# The game reads the keyboard's row 8 through the routine at 9FDDh, in the title
# and in play. It calls the routine above in place of IN A,(0AAh) / AND 0F0h.
patch page2:1FDD DB AA E6 F0 > CD D9 FC 00
