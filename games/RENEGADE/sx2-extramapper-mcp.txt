# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The title and the play take the interrupt in the game's routine at 0A38h,
# through JP at 0038h in its RAM page 0, which the code at 0A2Ah writes. The
# menu runs with the interrupts off: its loop at 6540h gives the border a
# random colour in each pass, about 3,000 times a second, too often for a call
# of SX2RES in each. So the image gets a wrapper for 0A38h at FC4Ch, and a
# second one at FC5Dh whose jump goes to the RET at FC84h, for a counter that
# the menu's loop calls. The play starts with 1, 2 or 3 in the menu, after a
# load from the tape.
rescall 0A38 FC84

# The two wrappers take 34 bytes, and the 24 bytes at FCC9h, before the tape
# service's part, hold one. FC4Ch is in BASIC's TRPTBL, zeros that the game
# leaves alone, also after the load of a level.
rescode FC4C

# The snapshot's JP 0A38h at 0038h, and LD HL,0A38h in the code that writes
# it, go to the first wrapper. With the rescall line's addresses, the build
# leaves that jump as it is.
patch page0:0039 38 0A > 4C FC
patch page0:0A2D 21 38 0A > 21 4C FC

# The counter at FC6Eh: AND 7 / CALL 09CEh, the menu's border, as RST 0 does,
# then one pass in 128 goes to the second wrapper, about 24 times a second,
# and the others return. FC85h holds the count.
patch page3:3C6E 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E6 07 CD CE 09 F5 3A 85 FC 3C
patch page3:3C78 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 32 85 FC E6 7F 20 04 F1 C3 5D
patch page3:3C82 00 00 00 > FC F1 C9

# AND 7 / RST 0 / NOP in the menu's loop becomes CALL 0FC6Eh / NOP. Both of
# the loop's ways reach it, after LD A,R or after LD A,0.
patch page1:258D E6 07 C7 00 > CD 6E FC 00
