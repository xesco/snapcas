# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper
# on and SX2RES running.

# After the snapshot the game puts its own routines into H.KEYI, over the
# image's call of SX2RES, which then stops: JP C800h written with LD HL,0C800h
# at ten places and JP C866h at the title, written with LD HL,0C866h at eight
# places. The image's wrappers call SX2RES and jump to each routine.
rescall C800 C866
