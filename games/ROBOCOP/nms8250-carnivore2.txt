# Lines for the Philips NMS 8250 with a Carnivore2 in slot 1.

# The game keeps its own data in F000h to FFFFh and a copy of the system area in
# 2000h to 2FFFh, and it copies that back to F000h to FFFFh before each load
# from the tape. On a machine whose RAM is in an expanded slot, FFFFh is the
# slot's subslot register, and a write there deselects the RAM. The NMS 8250 has
# no other RAM. The patches make the game's two fills of F000h to FFFFh and the
# copy from 2000h end at FFFEh. The second fill is rewritten to run down from
# FFFEh in the same 12 bytes.
patch side1:17FE 01 00 10 > 01 FF 0F
patch side1:1B7B 21 00 F0 3E FF 77 2C 20 FC 24 20 F9 > 21 FE FF 3E FF 77 2B CB 64 20 FA 00
patch side1:45F4 01 00 10 > 01 FF 0F
