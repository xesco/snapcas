# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The first binary, at BBC2h, sets RAMAD0 to RAMAD3 at F341h to FFh, then looks
# for RAM in pages 0 and 1 from slot ID 84h up with its own copy of ENASLT. It
# finds the Extra-Mapper in slot 0-1 and leaves it selected in pages 0 and 1 of
# slot 0's subslot register, so BASIC's ROM is gone when it returns. Here the
# BIOS has already set RAMAD0 to RAMAD3 to 84h, the slot that the search finds.
# The patches leave out the reset of RAMAD0 to RAMAD3 and the call of the
# search.
