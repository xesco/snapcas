# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The loader advent, at EF00h, copies the game's A-code to 0000h with pages 0
# and 1 in the slots of pages 2 and 3, by the slot register alone, and gives
# back the old value. Here BASIC and the RAM are both in slot 0, in subslots 0
# and 1, so the copy goes to the ROM. The patch rewrites the routine at EF34h
# to write 55h, RAM in every page, to slot 0's subslot register for the copy,
# and 50h, BASIC's subslots, after it.
patch side1:8B2 DB A8 F5 F3 E6 F0 4F DB A8 CB > F3 3E 55 32 FF FF 21 00 80 11
patch side1:8BC 2F CB 2F CB 2F CB 2F E6 0F B1 > 00 00 01 C0 63 ED B0 3E 50 32
patch side1:8C6 D3 A8 21 00 80 11 00 00 01 C0 > FF FF FB C9 00 00 00 00 00 00
patch side1:8D0 63 ED B0 F1 D3 A8 FB C9 > 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00

# The interpreter keeps the same two slot register values at 8072h and 8073h,
# and its routines at 8074h and 807Bh select BASIC and its RAM with them. The
# patches write 50h and 55h to the subslot register instead, and keep the
# block's sum.
patch side1:6E2C 3A 72 80 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
patch side1:6E34 3A 73 80 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:BAB8 92 > C4
