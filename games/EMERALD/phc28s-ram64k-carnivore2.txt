# The loader advent, at EF00h, copies the game's A-code to 0000h with pages 0
# and 1 in the slots of pages 2 and 3, and the game at 8000h selects the same
# slots to read it, with IN A,(A8h), four SRA As, AND 0Fh and OR C. With the
# Carnivore2, pages 2 and 3 are its mapper in slot 2-2, whose subslot pages 0
# and 1 do not select, and the interpreter answers "Acode?". The capture runs
# without it, where page 3 is the internal RAM in slot 0, which has the ROM in
# pages 0 and 1, so the page copy patch gives pages 0 and 1 the RAM cartridge,
# slot 1.
