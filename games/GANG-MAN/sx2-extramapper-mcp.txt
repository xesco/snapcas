# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The BASIC loader copies page 3's subslot into every page of slot 0's subslot
# register by a computed POKE -1, which gives 55h here, where the BIOS and the
# RAM share slot 0, and takes BASIC's ROM away from pages 0 and 1. The patch
# makes the computation keep pages 0 and 1 on the ROM: 50h.
patch side1:55 31 2E 30 36 32 35 > 31 2E 30 30 30 30

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image's call of SX2RES from H.KEYI
# never runs. A routine at FCD9h, in SLTATR's bytes of the empty slots 1 and 2,
# keeps IX, IY and the alternate registers, calls the image's routine at FD29h,
# enables the interrupts again, which that routine's RDSLT disables, and jumps
# to the BIOS's LDIRVM.
patch page3:3CD9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > DD E5 FD E5 D9 C5 D5 E5 08 F5
patch page3:3CE3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 08 D9 CD 29 FD D9 08 F1 08 E1
patch page3:3CED 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D1 C1 D9 FD E1 DD E1 FB C3 5C

# The routine at D34Bh copies the name table's buffer at E000h to the VRAM with
# LDIRVM once in each pass of the game's loop, in the title and in play. It
# calls the routine above in place of LDIRVM.
patch page3:1354 CD 5C 00 > CD D9 FC
