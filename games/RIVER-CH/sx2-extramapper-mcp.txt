# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image's call of SX2RES from H.KEYI
# never runs. A routine at FCD9h, in the zeros of SLTATR and SLTWRK, reads the
# VDP's status register 0, which the game never reads there. When its bit 7
# shows a new frame, it keeps IX, IY and the alternate registers and calls the
# image's routine at FD29h, so at most once a frame, and enables the interrupts
# again, which that routine's RDSLT disables. Two entries call it: the one at
# FCFDh then jumps to the BIOS's GTTRIG, the one at FD03h to the BIOS's WRTVRM.
patch page3:3CD9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 DB 99 07 30 1C DD E5 FD E5
patch page3:3CE3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D9 C5 D5 E5 08 F5 08 D9 CD 29
patch page3:3CED 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > FD D9 08 F1 08 E1 D1 C1 D9 FD
patch page3:3CF7 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > E1 DD E1 FB F1 C9 CD D9 FC C3
patch page3:3D01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D8 00 CD D9 FC C3 4D

# The title waits for a trigger in a loop that reads GTTRIG, thousands of times
# a second. Its first GTTRIG calls the entry at FCFDh.
patch page3:163D CD D8 00 > CD FD FC

# In play the routine at D1EAh writes the sprites' attributes with WRTVRM, about
# 50 times a second. Its first WRTVRM calls the entry at FD03h.
patch page3:11EF CD 4D 00 > CD 03 FD
