# Each run of the interpreter's init, as at a restart, fills the vector table
# at F200h and puts JP 0D31Ch, its interrupt routine, at F1F1h, in place of the
# vector that the build sends to its wrapper. So the image's wrapper at FCC9h
# calls SX2RES and jumps to that routine, and the patches below send the
# interrupt to the wrapper.
# The tape holds the next part after this one, so the image keeps the tape
# service, and the wrapper is at FCC9h.
rescall D31C

# The init's LD HL,0D31Ch, at CD6Bh, becomes LD HL,0FCC9h, the wrapper.
patch page3:D6C 1C D3 > C9 FC

# The snapshot's JP 0D31Ch at F1F1h jumps to the wrapper too.
