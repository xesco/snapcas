# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The loader's `slots` RAM search finds the RAM in 0-1 and leaves slot 0's
# subslot register at 55h, but its result and BASIC's slot register value are
# both 00h here, where the BIOS and the RAM share slot 0, so each later switch
# by the slot register alone does nothing. The patches skip the search and make
# each use of its stored values a write of slot 0's subslot register instead:
# 55h for RAM in every page, 54h for RAM in pages 1 to 3, 50h for BASIC.
patch side1:14D 3A A1 90 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:15D 3A A2 90 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
patch side1:168 3A A1 90 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:891 47 08 78 E6 > 4F 08 18 78
patch side1:917 3A FF FF 2F > 3E 54 00 00

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image has no call of SX2RES. The
# rescode line puts the image's call routine at EF70h, in zeros of page 3 that
# the game leaves alone, below its variable at EFF5h. The rescall line gives it
# three wrappers that call SX2RES: at EF9Dh, which jumps to EFB0h, at EFA3h,
# which jumps to the game's routine at 3EE3h, and at EFA9h, which jumps to the
# game's routine at 2506h. EFB0h does PUSH DE / LD DE,1F40h, the first
# instructions of the game's delay routine at 101Ch, and jumps to the rest of
# the routine at 1020h.
rescode EF70
rescall EFB0 3EE3 2506
patch page3:2FB0 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D5 11 40 1F C3 20 10

# The game waits with the delay routine at 101Ch, from 10 places. The routine
# jumps to the first wrapper in place of its first instructions.
patch page0:101C D5 11 40 > C3 9D EF

# The title's loop at 0BD7h reads the keys with the routine at 3EE3h and waits,
# about 10 times a second. It calls the second wrapper in place of that routine.
patch page0:BD7 CD E3 3E > CD A3 EF

# In play the routine at 2F1Eh sets the VRAM address with the routine at 2506h
# for each of the 14 rows that it draws, in bursts that come about 3 times a
# second, 47 calls a second in all. It calls the third wrapper in place of that
# routine.
patch page0:2F2A CD 06 25 > CD A9 EF
