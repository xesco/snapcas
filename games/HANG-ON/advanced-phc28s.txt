# Lines for the Sanyo PHC-28S with a RAM cartridge in slot 1 and a Carnivore2 in slot 2.

# The capture runs without the Carnivore2, so without a disk system, because the
# game crashes when one is present.
capture advanced/phc28s-nodisk

# Each part of the tape, made by CasLink3, gives pages 0 and 1 the slots of pages 2
# and 3 while it copies its piece of the game, with AND F0h, LD B,A, four RRCAs,
# AND 0Fh and OR B on the slot register's value. The last part then starts the
# game at 4000h or above. Without the Carnivore2, page 3 is the internal RAM in
# slot 0, which has the ROM in page 1, and the start lands in BASIC, which stops at
# "Syntax error". The patches load 15h in their place: slot 1, the RAM cartridge,
# in pages 0 to 2, and slot 0 in page 3.
patch side1:23A E6 F0 47 0F 0F 0F 0F E6 0F B0 > 3E 15 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
patch side1:433D E6 F0 47 0F 0F 0F 0F E6 0F B0 > 3E 15 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
