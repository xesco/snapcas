# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The capture runs on sx2-extramapper-noram30, without the 2048 KB mapper in
# 3-0. On sx2-extramapper-mcp itself the snapshot keeps a RAM page in 3-0, which
# SX2RES holds.
capture sx2-extramapper-noram30

# After the snapshot the game writes JP 4339h with LD HL,4339h at 4060h into
# H.KEYI, over the image's call of SX2RES, which then stops. The image's wrapper
# calls SX2RES and jumps to 4339h.
rescall 4339

# The game writes the wrapper's address, FD60h after resb at FD29h, into H.KEYI.
patch page1:61 39 43 > 60 FD
