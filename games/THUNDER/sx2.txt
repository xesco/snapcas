# it keeps RAM of its own in page 0, so the mode without a part in page 3, whose T0

# capture variant. At EDE1h the game wrote the part once a key started it, which
# the check, without keys, does not see. The part goes to EEE1h to EF7Eh, which
# hold zeros at the snapshot and in a minute of play with keys on
# sx2-extramapper-mcp.
