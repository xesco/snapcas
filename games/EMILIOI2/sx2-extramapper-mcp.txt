# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image has no call of SX2RES, and in
# play it writes a table over FD29h, the image's place by default. The rescode
# line puts the image's call routine at CC00h, in zeros of page 3 after the
# loader's messages that the game leaves alone. The rescall line gives it a
# wrapper at CC2Dh that calls SX2RES and jumps to CC40h, which does LD HL,8010h,
# the first instruction of the game's keyboard routine at 3317h, and jumps to
# the rest of the routine at 331Ah.
rescode CC00
rescall CC40
patch page3:C40 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 21 10 80 C3 1A 33

# In play the game reads the keyboard with the routine at 3317h, about 24 times
# a second. The routine jumps to the wrapper in place of its first instruction.
patch page0:3317 21 10 80 > C3 2D CC

# In play the game's stack is near FD67h, inside FD49h to FD88h, where the build
# puts the call's stack and its end. The stack goes to E800h, in zeros of page
# 3 that the game leaves alone in a minute of play with keys.
resstack E800

# The title calls no keyboard routine at 3317h: it loops at 314Ch, CALL 3154h /
# INC A / JR Z, which scans the keyboard thousands of times a second, so
# nothing called SX2RES there. A routine at CC50h, in zeros of page 3 that the
# game leaves alone, reads the VDP's status register 0, and when its bit 7
# shows a new frame it calls res3 at CC00h, so at most once a frame. It then
# jumps to 3154h. The loop's CALL calls it.
patch page3:C50 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > DB 99 07 DC 00 CC C3 54 31
patch page0:314C CD 54 31 > CD 50 CC
