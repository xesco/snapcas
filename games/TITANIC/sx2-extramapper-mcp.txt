# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.
# The game waits with a delay at E9D6h: 50 times LDIR from C000h to C000h,
# 3FFCh bytes, which writes each byte of page 3 with its own value, over any
# code of the image there, and the check fails point 2. The patch counts BC
# down from 3400h in place of each LDIR, a delay of the same length that
# writes nothing, and keeps A.
patch page3:29DC 01 FC 3F 21 00 C0 > F5 01 00 34 0B 78
patch page3:29E2 11 00 C0 ED B0 > B1 20 FB F1 00

# The game takes its interrupts at 0038h in its RAM of page 0, with JP 0F065h,
# and the image wraps that routine with res3 at FD29h. The game's stack is in
# page 1, which res3 selects for SX2RES, so res3 skipped every call. A routine
# at F9A0h, in zeros of page 3 that the game leaves alone in a minute of play
# with keys, calls res3 on a stack of its own below D0A0h, in zeros the game
# leaves alone too, and jumps to F065h.
patch page3:39A0 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 73 FE F9 31 A0 D0 CD 29 FD
patch page3:39AA 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 7B FE F9 C3 65 F0

# The jump at 0038h goes to the routine at F9A0h, and the image's wrapper then
# goes to it in turn.
patch page0:39 65 F0 > A0 F9
