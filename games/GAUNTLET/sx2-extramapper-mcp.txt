# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The main game takes the interrupt for a routine of its own, at B45Bh, with
# JP 0B45Bh at 0038h in its RAM in page 0, so nothing calls SX2RES then. The
# image's wrapper at FCC9h calls SX2RES and jumps to that routine.
rescall B45B

# The main game puts the wrapper's address at 0039h: LD HL,0B45Bh becomes
# LD HL,0FCC9h. The block is unread at the snapshot.
patch side1:D2A0 5B B4 > C9 FC

# The block's 16-bit sum, after game.txt's line for it, with the B6h that the
# patch above adds.
patch side1:E2F3 39 F3 > EF F3
