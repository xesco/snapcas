# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# NAKAL3's loader fills the hooks with RET from FD9Fh, by LD HL,0FD9Fh / LD
# DE,0FDA0h / LD BC,0258h / LD (HL),0C9h / LDIR, to FFF6h, over an MSX2's
# copies of VDP registers 8 to 23 at FFE7h to FFF6h, so the machine resets on
# every MSX2. The capture's hook fill patch knows only a fill from FD9Ah. The
# patch gives the fill the count 022Ah, which ends it at FFC9h.
patch side1:1A248 01 58 02 > 01 2A 02

# Super MSX's loaders find RAM for pages 0 and 1 by the slot register alone, keep
# the values at FFFDh and FFFEh, and select them later by the slot register
# again. Here the BIOS and the RAM are both in slot 0, in subslots 0 and 1, so
# the patches write slot 0's subslot register instead: 54h for RAM in pages 1
# to 3, 55h for RAM in every page, 50h for BASIC, with SLTTBL's entry for slot 0
# at FCC5h where a ROM image looks up its slot, and 84h as RAM's slot ID.
patch side1:BB7E 26 40 C6 04 D3 A8 4F 7E 2F 77 BE 79 20 F4 32 FE FF 01 00 20 21 00 A0 11 00 40 ED B0 > 3A FF FF 2F F5 3E 54 32 FF FF 01 00 20 21 00 A0 11 00 40 ED B0 F1 32 FF FF 00 00 00
patch side1:F802 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 54 32 FF FF
patch side1:F812 DB A8 E6 F0 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 00
patch side1:15833 26 00 C6 01 D3 A8 4F 7E 2F 77 BE 79 20 F4 26 40 C6 04 D3 A8 4F 7E 2F 77 BE 79 20 F4 32 FD FF E6 FC 32 FE FF 21 00 90 11 00 00 01 00 40 ED B0 > 3A FF FF 2F F5 3E 55 32 FF FF 21 00 90 11 00 00 01 00 40 ED B0 F1 32 FF FF 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00
patch side1:1A261 3A FD FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:1A271 DB A8 E6 F0 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 00

# After the snapshot the game puts its own routines into H.KEYI, over the
# image's call of SX2RES, which then stops: JP C800h written with LD HL,0C800h
# at ten places and JP C866h at the title, written with LD HL,0C866h at eight
# places. The image's wrappers call SX2RES and jump to each routine.
