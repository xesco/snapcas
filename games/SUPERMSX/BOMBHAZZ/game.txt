# The loader ends with EI and JP 0100h, and the game's code there starts with 14
# NOPs and then LD HL,25F6h and LD (0039h),HL, which give its interrupt routine's
# JP at 0038h, which the loader writes, its address. A VDP interrupt that is
# pending at the EI, as it is on an MSX2 after the loader's SCREEN 2 with its
# interrupts off, comes before that, and the JP goes to the bytes that the RAM
# holds at 0039h. The first patch makes the EI a NOP, and the second makes the
# first 7 NOPs LD HL,25F6h, LD (0039h),HL and EI. The game then sets the address
# again, as before.
patch side1:4A3A FB > 00
patch side1:896 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 21 F6 25 22 39 00 FB
