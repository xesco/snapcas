# Lines for the Philips NMS 8250 with a Carnivore2 in slot 1.

# The capture runs without the Carnivore2, because the game fails in a capture
# with it in slot 1.
capture nms8250

# The game moves 4000h to BFFFh up to 8000h to FFFFh, by an LDDR of 8001h bytes
# from C000h to 0000h. On a machine whose RAM is in an expanded slot, FFFFh is
# the slot's subslot register, and the move's second byte deselects the RAM. The
# NMS 8250 has no other RAM. The patch makes the move 7FFFh bytes, from BFFEh to
# FFFEh. The two bytes left out are those for 0000h and for FFFFh.
patch side1:3A16 21 00 C0 11 00 00 01 01 80 > 21 FE BF 11 FE FF 01 FF 7F

# The loader keeps an exclusive OR of the block's bytes and compares it with the
# byte after the block. When they differ, it flashes the Caps lamp 16 times and
# starts the game 5 seconds later. The second patch gives that byte the value for
# the changed block, CCh XOR 81h.
patch side1:F6B5 CC > 4D
