# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# Super MSX's loader `slots` finds RAM by the slot register alone, keeps BASIC's
# slot register value at FFFDh and the RAM's at FFFEh, and selects them later by
# the slot register again. Here the BIOS and the RAM share slot 0, so both
# values are 00h and each switch does nothing. The patches skip the search and
# make each use of the two values a write of slot 0's subslot register
# instead: 50h for BASIC and 55h for RAM in every page.
patch side1:71D 47 08 78 E6 > 4F 08 18 78
patch side1:799 20 DA > 18 0B
patch side1:87D 3A FD FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:892 3A FE FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:8CE 3A FD FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:8FF 3A FE FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:945 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:8A6F 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:8AB8 3A FD FF D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
patch side1:CBE3 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF

# After its copy to 4000h with the RAM in every page, the loader gives pages 0
# and 1 back to BASIC with AND 0F0h / OUT (0A8h),A on the RAM's value, and a
# later routine does the same from FFFEh. With 55h that writes 50h to the slot
# register, slot 1 in pages 2 and 3, which holds nothing. The patches write 50h
# to slot 0's subslot register instead, with the NOP after the first OUT.
patch side1:955 E6 F0 D3 A8 00 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
patch side1:8A7F 3A FE FF E6 F0 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 00 00

# The game writes over the tape service's part of page 3 at its default place.
# The part goes to D3E1h to D47Eh, as on sx2, which hold zeros at the snapshot
# and in the check's minute of play.
service at D3E1

# The game takes its interrupts at 0038h in its RAM in page 0, with PUSH AF / IN
# A,(99h) / POP AF / JP 9A97h, so nothing called SX2RES. It also uses the zeros
# at FCC9h in page 3, where the image's wrapper for the call goes with the tape
# service. The wrapper goes to D500h, in zeros of page 3 that the game leaves
# alone in a minute of play with keys. It calls SX2RES and jumps to 9A97h.
rescode D500
rescall 9A97

# The jump at 003Ch goes to the wrapper.
patch page0:3D 97 9A > 00 D5
