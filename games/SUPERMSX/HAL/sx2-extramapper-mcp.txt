# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# Super MSX's loader `slots` finds RAM by the slot register alone, keeps BASIC's
# slot register value at FFFDh and the RAM's at FFFEh, and selects them later by
# the slot register again. Here the BIOS and the RAM share slot 0, so both
# values are 00h and each switch does nothing. The patches skip the search and
# make each use of the two values a write of slot 0's subslot register
# instead: 50h for BASIC and 55h for RAM in every page.
patch side1:AA20 47 08 78 E6 > 4F 08 18 78
patch side1:AA9C 20 DA > 18 0B
patch side1:AB80 3A FD FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:AB95 3A FE FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:ABD1 3A FD FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:AC02 3A FE FF 20 04 D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF 18 05
patch side1:AC48 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:EA72 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF
patch side1:12B9B 3A FD FF D3 A8 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
patch side1:16CC6 3A FE FF D3 A8 > 3E 55 32 FF FF

# After each copy to 4000h with the RAM in every page, the loader gives pages 0
# and 1 back to BASIC with AND 0F0h / OUT (0A8h),A on the RAM's value. With 55h
# that writes 50h to the slot register, slot 1 in pages 2 and 3, which holds
# nothing. The patches write 50h to slot 0's subslot register instead, with the
# NOP after the OUT.
patch side1:AC58 E6 F0 D3 A8 00 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
patch side1:EA82 E6 F0 D3 A8 00 > 3E 50 32 FF FF
