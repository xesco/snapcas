# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# The loader at DD00h is Engesoft's, as in games/ENGESOFT/GALAXIAN. Its RAM
# search at DE43h tries the slot IDs 3, 2 and 1 only, never an expanded slot.
# Here the RAM is in slot 0-1, ID 84h. The patch stores 84h as the slot of
# pages 0 and 1, at DD3Eh and DD3Fh, and returns.
patch side1:1880C 21 10 00 22 3A DD 3E 03 32 > 3E 84 32 3E DD 32 3F DD C9

# After the copy to page 1, the loader gives page 1 slot ID 00h again, by XOR
# A at DD44h, for BASIC's ROM. Here ID 00h changes only the slot register and
# leaves page 1 on the RAM, because slot 0 is expanded. The patches put LD
# A,80h / LD H,40h / JR 0DD47h, the ROM's ID 80h, in the NOPs at DD34h, and
# make the loader return there in place of DD44h.
patch side1:186FD 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 3E 80 26 40 18 0D

patch side1:186DE 21 44 DD > 21 34 DD
