# The game's interrupt routine is at 0038h itself, in its RAM in page 0, so
# nothing called SX2RES. Its every path ends with EI / RETI at 0100h, which
# becomes a jump to the image's wrapper at FCC9h. The wrapper calls SX2RES and
# jumps to EI / RET at 07E1h, the end of one of the game's routines.
