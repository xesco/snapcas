# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image's call of SX2RES from T0 never
# runs. The image's wrapper at FCC9h, before the tape service's entries, calls
# SX2RES and jumps to a RET at FC5Fh.
rescall FC5F

# Two entries at FC4Ch, in BASIC's zeros of TRPTBL, which the game leaves alone.
# The first counts its calls in the byte at FC67h and calls the wrapper at every
# 256th; the second calls it each time. Each then does the instructions that its
# call replaces and returns.
patch page3:3C4C 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F5 3A 67 FC C6 01 32 67 FC 30
patch page3:3C56 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 03 CD C9 FC F1 FD 21 70 D0 C9
patch page3:3C60 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > CD C9 FC CD C9 4C C9

# The title plays its music in a loop at 5AEDh, about 7000 times a second, until
# a key. It calls the first entry in place of LD IY,0D070h, about 25 times a
# second.
patch page1:1AED FD 21 70 D0 > CD 4C FC 00

# The game's loop at 4BB6h calls the routine at 4CC9h first in each pass, about
# 30 times a second. It calls the second entry instead, which calls that routine
# after the wrapper.
patch page1:BB6 CD C9 4C > CD 60 FC
