# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game takes the interrupt in routines of its own: its routine at 8C91h
# writes JP HL into H.KEYI, over the image's CALL resb, so SX2RES got no more
# calls once play started. It installs 87F7h from 87F0h, and 9CF9h from 98A9h
# and 9C40h. The image gets a wrapper for each that calls SX2RES and jumps to
# the routine, at FD00h for 87F7h and FD06h for 9CF9h, and each LD HL takes
# the wrapper's address.
rescall 87F7 9CF9
patch page2:07F1 F7 87 > 00 FD
patch page2:18AA F9 9C > 06 FD
patch page2:1C41 F9 9C > 06 FD
