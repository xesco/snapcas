# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The title runs without interrupts, and the game takes them in play in
# interrupt mode 2, with its stack in page 1 throughout, where SX2RES is, so a
# call there is left out. In play the game fills F200h to FFF0h with 70h and
# E200h to EFF0h with zeros, over the image's call at FD29h, and the interrupt
# that the build sends to its wrapper there crashed. The image's code for the
# call, its call routine res3 and its wrapper at D9B3h, which calls res3 and
# jumps to a RET at DA14h, goes to D986h instead, in zeros below the game's
# fills.
rescode D986
rescall DA14

# An entry at D9D9h for the interrupt keeps the game's stack pointer at D9EAh,
# takes a stack below DAC0h, in the same zeros, calls the wrapper, takes the
# game's stack back and jumps to the game's routine at 5A5Ah.
patch page3:19D9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 73 EA D9 31 C0 DA CD B3 D9
patch page3:19E3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > ED 7B EA D9 C3 5A 5A

# The game's vector table at 5800h, for its I of 58h, holds 5Ah, so the CPU
# calls 5A5Ah. The vector at 58FFh, which the CPU reads with FFh on the data
# bus, as on an MSX, goes to the entry at D9D9h instead.
patch page1:18FF 5A 5A > D9 D9

# An entry at D9ECh disables the interrupts, as the title's code does next,
# counts its calls in the bytes from DA17h and at every 1024th keeps the game's
# stack pointer at DA15h, takes the stack below DAC0h, calls the wrapper and
# takes the game's stack back. Then it does LD A,0F0h / DI and returns.
patch page3:19EC 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > F3 F5 3A 17 DA C6 01 32 17 DA
patch page3:19F6 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 30 18 3A 18 DA C6 40 32 18 DA
patch page3:1A00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 30 0E ED 73 15 DA 31 C0 DA CD
patch page3:1A0A 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > B3 D9 ED 7B 15 DA F1 3E F0 F3
patch page3:1A14 00 > C9

# The title waits for 1, 2 or 3 in a loop at 99BAh that reads the keyboard's row
# 0, about 30000 times a second. It calls the entry in place of LD A,0F0h / DI.
patch page2:19BA 3E F0 F3 > CD EC D9

# When play starts, the game writes FD71h in SLTWRK over res3's default stack.
# Put the stack in unused zeros at F9A0h.
resstack D9C0
