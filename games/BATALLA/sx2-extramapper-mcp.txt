# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on
# and SX2RES running.

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image's call of SX2RES from H.KEYI
# never runs. A routine at FCD9h, in the zeros of SLTATR and SLTWRK, keeps IX,
# IY and the alternate registers and calls the image's routine at FD29h, and
# enables the interrupts again, which that routine's RDSLT disables. Two entries
# call it: the one at FCF6h then jumps to the game's routine at B582h, the one
# at FCFCh to the BIOS's GTSTCK.
patch page3:3CD9 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > DD E5 FD E5 D9 C5 D5 E5 08 F5
patch page3:3CE3 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > 08 D9 CD 29 FD D9 08 F1 08 E1
patch page3:3CED 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D1 C1 D9 FD E1 DD E1 FB C9 CD
patch page3:3CF7 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 > D9 FC C3 82 B5 CD D9 FC C3 D5

# In the title the routine at CD68h waits for the VDP's frame with RDVDP and
# then calls the routine at B582h. It calls the entry at FCF6h in place of that
# routine, once a frame.
patch page3:D72 CD 82 B5 > CD F6 FC

# In play the routine at B44Dh reads the stick with GTSTCK, about 30 times a
# second. It calls the entry at FCFCh in place of GTSTCK.
patch page2:3455 CD D5 00 > CD FC FC
