# Lines for sx2-extramapper-mcp, the SX-2 (MSX2+, 6144 KB) with its Extra-Mapper on and SX2RES running.

# For its double-width text the game builds a table of 256 words in FE00h to
# FFFFh, at 9F16h, and reads it at B739h with twice the byte as the offset. On a
# machine whose RAM is in an expanded slot, FFFFh is the slot's subslot
# register, and the last word deselects the RAM. Here all RAM is in slot 0-1.
# The patches put the table two bytes lower, in FDFEh to FFFDh. The builder
# starts at FDFEh. The reader's six bytes become LD L,A, LD H,7Fh, DEC HL,
# ADD HL,HL and NOP, which give FDFEh plus twice the byte. FDFEh and FDFFh hold
# 00h in every block of the tape that loads them, after a table that ends at
# FDFDh, and the table's first word, for the byte 00h, is 0000h. The last patch
# gives the byte after the block the exclusive OR of the changed block, which
# the loader checks.
patch side1:81D7 21 00 FE > 21 FE FD
patch side1:99FA 87 6F CE FE 95 67 > 6F 26 7F 2B 29 00
patch side1:E0D9 18 > FB

# The game runs without interrupts, so the image has no call of SX2RES. The
# rescode line puts the image's two wrappers at D3A0h, in zeros of page 3 that
# the game leaves alone, before the tape service's part at D3E1h. The wrapper at
# D3A0h calls SX2RES and jumps to the game's routine at A21Ch, the one at D3B1h
# to its routine at 9D01h. The wrappers disable the interrupts, which the game
# takes none of.
rescode D3A0
rescall A21C 9D01

# The title's loop at 0679h calls the routine at A21Ch from 069Ah, about 9 times
# a second, and in play the routine at C676h calls the routine at 9D01h about 16
# times a second. Both call the wrappers in place of those routines.
patch page0:69A CD 1C A2 > CD A0 D3
patch page3:67B CD 01 9D > CD B1 D3
